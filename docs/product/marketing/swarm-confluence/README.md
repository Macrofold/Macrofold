# Swarm Confluence

The separate collection at `/swarm-confluence/index.html` develops [Continuum](../swarm-continuum/README.md) with 80,000 particles, fuller surfaces, and definition that increases toward dispersal. Earlier collections and source archives remain preserved.

## Surfaces and motion

The additional particles primarily cover the surfaces. Polyhedral studies reserve 12% of their population for edge samples, while face particles retain full brightness; a small inner population supplies depth. Material travel covers both sides of each surface without lingering at its poles. All particle identities continue moving while the geometry changes.

From approximately six to fifteen seconds, independent surface scatter settles and regional order strengthens. The wandering population becomes quieter while the curved inlet remains readable. Maximum definition arrives around 14.8 seconds, before the wind release starts. Large sections keep morphing throughout; increasing definition does not freeze their geometry.

The incoming lattice remains a shallow curved plane. Particles fade in across roughly its first 70%, leaving the latter columns fully visible before gradually losing their order. The release retains velocity and disperses in varied directions; ordinary departing particles also fade continuously before recycling.

## Ten studies

Viscera, Chitin, and Alveoli retain their anatomical geometry. Thalassa and Maelstrom replace Rhizome and Heterodyne with two different currents:

- **Thalassa:** twelve broad, layered pressure fronts carry slow traveling curves. The current widens downstream and during growth.
- **Maelstrom:** twelve thick fronts curl around three unequal, counter-rotating regions, with stronger traveling crests and changing width.
- **Druse:** eighty-four small crystals grow across seven groups of staggered terraces. Their facets, inclinations, and arrangements keep changing; this replaces Dendrite.

Tetrarch, Hypercell, Coronet, and Aperiodic retain their changing assemblies and receive the shared surface treatment. Five organic and five polyhedral studies remain available one at a time.

## Playback and delivery

The studio opens paused at fourteen seconds. Drag the timeline and press **Play** to continue from that point. The speed slider retains its 1.0–2.0× range in 0.1 increments and preserves time when changed. The twenty-second sequence keeps formation through six seconds, morphing until fifteen, wind dispersal through nineteen, and one transparent second before reset.

The [renderer](../../../../apps/web/public/swarm-confluence/renderer.mjs), [geometry](../../../../apps/web/public/swarm-confluence/shapes.mjs), and [studio](../../../../apps/web/public/swarm-confluence/studio.mjs) use native WebGL, GLSL, and browser APIs without runtime dependencies. Adaptive resolution retains all 80,000 draw identities. Orthographic projection keeps point size and brightness independent of camera distance; there is no invisible depth mesh. These are art-directed procedural surfaces and shared motion fields, not a physical simulation of neighbor forces or a count of real agents.

Transparent 2880 × 1920 PNGs and 1440 × 960 WebP posters accompany the live animation.
