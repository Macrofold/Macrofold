# Swarm Efflorescence

The separate collection at `/swarm-efflorescence/index.html` develops [Confluence](../swarm-confluence/README.md) with a longer inlet fade, earlier surface definition, connected crystalline growth, and an expanding branching study. Confluence and every earlier collection remain preserved.

## Formation and definition

The curved inlet fades from transparent to visible over twice the previous distance: 640 composition units instead of 320. Organization cannot bypass that fade. The grid gradually loses its order as regional surfaces develop; the open current studies begin material travel earlier in that transition to avoid gathering at a shared lip.

All 80,000 particle identities remain active. Fine displacement settles and surface coherence strengthens from 5.5 to eleven seconds. Full definition then continues for five seconds while both the material flow and larger geometry keep changing. Extra points cover surfaces, with a smaller population defining polyhedral edges and interior depth.

The sequence lasts twenty-one seconds: formation develops through six, definition matures through eleven, peak morphing continues through sixteen, wind dispersal finishes by twenty, and one transparent second resets the loop. The additional second preserves the four-second release and five-second peak interval. Particles carry their velocity into varied dispersal directions.

## Eleven studies

Six organic and five polyhedral studies appear one at a time. Viscera, Chitin, Alveoli, Tetrarch, Hypercell, and Coronet retain their geometry and receive the shared timing and fade treatment.

- **Thalassa:** twelve open wave fronts widen directly into the current, with varied traveling crests and layered depths.
- **Maelstrom:** three rolling current families retain their twisting focal regions while their curved surfaces and troughs become clearer.
- **Plexus:** sixty-three connected tube segments across six branching levels grow outward while their shared junctions bend. Growth extends the tips toward the composition edges.
- **Druse:** 168 small crystals, twice Confluence's count, grow across seven aggregates with four terraces of six crystals each.
- **Aperiodic:** 32 primary crystals emerge from one changing faceted core; each carries two attached daughter crystals. Its 96 crystals grow and shrink continuously, with several long spires approaching thirty percent of the composition height. Four additional material sectors sample the common core.

## Playback and delivery

The studio opens paused at fourteen seconds. Drag the timeline and press **Play** to continue from that point. The speed slider retains its 1.0–2.0× range in 0.1 increments and preserves time when changed. **Play formation** stops at six seconds; **Play 21 s loop** repeats the complete sequence. Phase labels use animation time, independent of playback speed.

The [renderer](../../../../apps/web/public/swarm-efflorescence/renderer.mjs), [geometry](../../../../apps/web/public/swarm-efflorescence/shapes.mjs), and [studio](../../../../apps/web/public/swarm-efflorescence/studio.mjs) use native WebGL, GLSL, and browser APIs without runtime dependencies. Adaptive resolution retains the full particle population. Orthographic projection keeps particle size and brightness independent of camera distance; no invisible depth mesh masks another layer. These are art-directed procedural surfaces and shared motion fields, not a physical neighbor-force simulation or a count of real agents.
